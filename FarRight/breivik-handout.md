# Primary Source Handout: Anders Breivik, *2083: A European Declaration of Independence* (2011)

**For classroom use -- selected passages with editorial framing**

*Anders Behring Breivik (writing as "Andrew Berwick") killed 77 people in Norway on July 22, 2011 -- eight in a government quarter bombing in Oslo, and 69 at a youth camp on Utoya island. He distributed this 1,518-page document electronically on the day of the attacks. It has been cited and excerpted in manifestos and communications by subsequent far-right attackers in multiple countries. The Norwegian court found him legally sane. He was sentenced to 21 years in preventive detention.*

*The passages below are selected to illustrate his ideology and intellectual influences. We read them as primary source material -- not to evaluate the validity of any claim, but to understand the structure of his thinking.*

---

*From the opening address to the reader (Book 1):*

> "After years of work the first edition of the compendium '2083 -- A European Declaration of Independence' is completed. If you have received this book, you are either one of my former 7000 patriotic Facebook friends or you are the friend of one of my FB friends...
>
> Much of the information presented in this compendium has been deliberately kept away from the European peoples by our governments and the politically correct mainstream media. More than 90% of the EU and national parliamentarians and more than 95% of journalists are supporters of European multiculturalism and therefore supporters of the ongoing Islamic colonization of Europe; yet, they DO NOT have the permission of the European peoples to implement these doctrines.
>
> As we all know, the root of Europe's problems is the lack of cultural self-confidence (nationalism). Most people are still terrified of nationalistic political doctrines thinking that if we ever embrace these principles again, new 'Hitlers' will suddenly pop up and initiate global Armageddon... Needless to say, the growing numbers of nationalists in Western Europe are systematically being ridiculed, silenced and persecuted by the current cultural Marxist/multiculturalist political establishments. This has been a continuous ongoing process which started in 1945. This irrational fear of nationalistic doctrines is preventing us from stopping our own national/cultural suicide as the Islamic colonization is increasing annually.
>
> I am 100% certain that the distribution of this compendium to a large portion of European patriots will contribute to ensure our victory in the end. Because within these three books lies the tools required to win the ongoing Western European cultural war."

## Passage 2: The Enemy -- "Cultural Marxism" and the Frankfurt School

*From Book 1, compiling and synthesizing material from the Free Congress Foundation (an American paleoconservative think-tank). This section is typical of Breivik's method: he assembles and reframes existing far-right intellectual material.*

> "The Frankfurt School's studies combined Marxist analysis with Freudian psychoanalysis to form the basis of what became known as 'Critical Theory.' Critical Theory, as it came to be called, was essentially destructive criticism of the main elements of Western culture, including Christianity, capitalism, authority, the family, patriarchy, hierarchy, morality, tradition, sexual restraint, loyalty, patriotism, nationalism, heredity, ethnocentrism, convention and conservatism. These criticisms were reflected in such works as Erich Fromm's *Escape from Freedom* and *The Art of Loving*, Wilhelm Reich's *The Mass Psychology of Fascism*, and Herbert Marcuse's *Eros and Civilisation* and *One Dimensional Man*...
>
> Gertrude Himmelfarb has observed that it slipped past traditional academics almost unobserved until it was too late. It occurred so 'quietly' that when they 'looked up,' postmodernism was upon them with a vengeance. They were surrounded by such a tidal wave of multicultural subjects such as radical feminism, deconstructed relativism as history and other courses which undermine the perpetuation of Western civilisation. Indeed, this tidal wave slipped by just as Antonio Gramsci and the Frankfurt School had envisioned -- a quiet revolution propagating a European hate ideology with the goal of destroying Western civilisation...
>
> 'Cultural Marxism,' as preached by the Frankfurt School has thus spurred the widely popular and destructive concepts of 'affirmative action,' 'multiculturalism' and 'diversity.' One can't escape these terms today. These concepts have destroyed every defensive structure of European society which has laid the foundation for the Islamisation of Europe."

**[Discussion prompt: What is the relationship between the "internal enemy" (cultural Marxists) and the "external enemy" (Islamic immigration) in Breivik's framework? Compare to classical fascism's internal/external enemy pairing. Note that the Frankfurt School thinkers he names are largely Jewish -- what historical parallel does this suggest?]**

## Passage 3: The Self -- "Justiciar Knight"

*From Book 3, Breivik's framework for individual actors. He is describing himself and the role he believes he is authorized to play:*

> "Any self appointed Justiciar Knight has been given the authority, by [the Knights Templar order] on behalf of:
> 1. The free indigenous peoples of Europe
> 2. Those Europeans not yet born
> 3. The legacy of our forefathers and fallen martyrs
>
> ...to act as a judge, jury and executioner until the free, indigenous peoples of Europe are no longer threatened by cultural genocide...
>
> The Order and Tribunal has concluded that any and all Europeans have not just a right, but a duty to resist through political and military means; cultural Marxist/multiculturalist atrocities and crimes committed against the indigenous peoples of Europe. As such, any European Christian conservative can act as a Justiciar Knight. This includes Christian agnostics and Christian atheists. Although the [order] is a pan-European indigenous rights movement we give all Europeans, regardless of skin colour, the opportunity to become a Justiciar Knight as long as the individual is either a Christian, Christian agnostic or a Christian atheist."

And from his statement of objectives:

> "We have come to the conclusion that the democratical struggle to save Europe has been lost. Our purpose and goal is to defeat the cultural Marxist/Multiculturalist Alliance of Europe, seize political and military control of Western European countries and implement a cultural conservative political agenda."

**[Discussion prompt: Breivik explicitly rejects biological racism -- "regardless of skin colour." How does this departure from classical fascism shape his ideology? What does the "judge, jury and executioner" formulation tell us about how he understands political authority and legitimacy? What does "democratical struggle has been lost" signal about the radicalization logic?]**

## Passage 4: The Title -- 2083

*The manifesto's title refers to a date, not a page count.*

> "2083 -- the 400th anniversary of the Battle of Vienna, 1683, when Ottoman forces besieging the city were repelled by a combined European army under the Polish King John Sobieski."

Breivik projects that by 2083, the "Western European Resistance" will have completed what he calls "Phase 3" of a civil war -- the full removal of Muslim populations from Europe and the trial and punishment of "cultural Marxist traitors." The three-phase timeline runs:

> "Phase 1 -- 1999-2030 [awareness and 'shock attacks']
> Phase 2 -- 2030-2070
> Phase 3 -- 2070-2083"

**[Discussion prompt: What does the choice of 1683 as the founding reference point tell us about the kind of historical narrative Breivik is constructing? Compare to Stanley's "mythic past" tactic and Finchelstein's account of fascism's relationship to national/civilizational origin myths. Note the century-long timeline -- what does the scale of this vision suggest about the genre of thinking Breivik is engaged in?]**

## Reading Notes for Instructors

**On style:** Breivik writes in a flat, bureaucratic register that oscillates between pseudo-academic citation (he names Frankfurt School figures, cites Gramsci, references specific studies) and grandiose declarations about civilizational destiny. This combination is analytically important: the scholarly apparatus gives the ideology a veneer of evidential grounding while the underlying claims are conspiracy theory. Students often react to this style with either dismissal ("he sounds insane") or uncomfortable recognition ("I've seen some of these talking points before"). Both reactions are worth pausing on.

**On compilation:** Book 1 is largely assembled from existing texts -- the Free Congress Foundation's pamphlets on political correctness, the Counterjihad blogosphere, paleoconservative American writers. Breivik did not invent "cultural Marxism" as a concept; he synthesized it. The manifesto is more an aggregation node than an original work.

**On the internal/external enemy structure:** The architecture is: (1) Europe has an authentic indigenous people; (2) that people is under existential threat from Islamic immigration; (3) the immigration is not an accident but a deliberate policy engineered by "cultural Marxist" elites who are the primary enemy; (4) therefore the first target of violence must be the internal traitors, not Muslim immigrants directly. This explains Utoya -- he attacked a Labour Party youth camp, not a mosque.

**On influence:** Passages from this manifesto, sometimes verbatim, appear in the manifestos of the Christchurch shooter (2019, 51 killed), the El Paso shooter (2019, 23 killed), and others. The document functions as a genre-establishing text for what researchers call "accelerationist" lone-wolf terrorism.
