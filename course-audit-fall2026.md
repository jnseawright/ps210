## 🟡 MEDIUM PRIORITY — Significant pedagogical improvements

### 7. Add explicit learning objectives to lecture slide decks
Labs 1 and 2 both open with a clear "Today's Goals" list, which is good pedagogy. Most lecture slides don't have this. Students benefit from knowing upfront what they should be able to do after each session. Adding 3–5 objectives at the start of each lecture deck (especially `Causation.Rmd`, `Surveys.Rmd`, `Experiments.Rmd`, `ConfidenceIntervals.Rmd`, `Regression.Rmd`, `qualitative.Rmd`) would improve student engagement and exam preparation.


### 12. Add accessibility descriptions to image-heavy slides
`ethics.Rmd` in particular consists almost entirely of `![image](images/filename.png)` slides with no surrounding text. If images don't load, or a student uses assistive technology, the lecture is unreadable. Even a one-sentence caption explaining what each screenshot shows would help significantly. This also makes slides more useful as study materials for students reviewing after class.
